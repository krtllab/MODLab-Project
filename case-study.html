Visceral Roots Archive

A complete visual identity and artwork package designed for a dark fantasy streetwear brand, bringing hand-inked macabre illustrations to modern apparel.

Client

The Undead Root

Timeline

4 Weeks (2025)

Role

Lead Illustrator & Designer

Deliverables

Graphics & Mockups

The Challenge

The client needed a striking visual identity for their flagship streetwear line that avoided generic stock graphics. They required original, highly intricate hand-drawn illustrations that appealed to heavy metal enthusiasts, dark fantasy collectors, and modern streetwear aficionados.

The key challenge was ensuring that fine ink lines and complex shading translated seamlessly to screen printing on heavyweight cotton garments without losing detail or vibrancy.

The Process & Solution

01

Pencil & Ink Concepting

Developing raw pencil sketches, focusing on bone structures, visceral roots, and gothic typography balance.

02

High-Res Vector Separation

Digitizing ink drawings at 600 DPI and separating line art layers for screen-printing halftones.
